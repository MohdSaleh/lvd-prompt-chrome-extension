Usability & Ergonomic Deficits
  • Recursive Mutation: In sequential replacement, sub-tokens corrupted already replaced sentences.
  • Static Interface: Popup lacked playground, instant dictionary lookup, or custom rule controls.
  • Restricted Editor: Legacy options editor was locked to a 220px fixed textarea.
  • Low Contrast: Failed WCAG accessibility guidelines with low text contrast.

Minimalist Architectural Principles
  • Single-Pass Non-Recursive Compiler: Range-based mathematical replacement guarantees zero corruption.
  • Quiet Monochromatic Palette: Pure neutral zinc and obsidian without colored visual clutter or tacky gradients.
  • Sleek Architectural Split Workbench: Synchronized dual-pane editor with live token estimation.
  • Subtle Micro-Interactions: Clean pill roving navigation (Tab, Alt+Arrows) and reversible undo (Alt+Z).
